परिचयः/ About Me

नमस्ते! स्वागतम्, I am an Assistant Professor at Central Sanskrit University, Shri Raghunath Kirti Campus, Devprayag. I hold a Ph.D. in Nyāya Darśana and specialize in traditional Sanskrit systems of logic, classical Indian epistemology, and text digitization methodologies. My academic work spans ancient textual analyses alongside modern computational approaches to Indian knowledge systems.

✨ Traditional & Śāstric Lineage: Actively pursued deep text-based shastric education at the prestigious Rajiv Gandhi Campus, Shringeri, Karnataka (Rashtriya Sanskrit Sansthan) for seven years, specializing fundamentally in classical Nyāya, Vyākaraṇa, and Advaita Vedānta systems.

Languages: Sanskrit, Hindi, English, Kannada, Punjabi
